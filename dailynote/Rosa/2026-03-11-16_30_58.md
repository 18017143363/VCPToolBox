[16:36] npm_ops.rs prebuild镜像环境变量名bug修复
截图诊断发现 npm报 `Unknown env config "better-sqlite3-binary-host-mirror"`
根因：环境变量名 `npm_config_better_sqlite3_binary_host_mirror` 多了 `_mirror` 后缀
npm把npm_config_前缀去掉后当自己的配置项解析但不认识→prebuild-install没收到镜像地址→从GitHub下载失败→fallback到node-gyp→缺MSVC→失败
修复：`npm_config_better_sqlite3_binary_host_mirror` → `npm_config_better_sqlite3_binary_host`
prebuild-install标准查找键是binary_host不是binary_host_mirror
