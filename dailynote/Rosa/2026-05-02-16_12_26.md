## 两个命令
1. GPTGenerateImage(文生图): prompt(必需)+size(最高4K 3840px)+quality(low/medium/high/auto)+background(transparent/opaque/auto)+n(1-4张批量)
2. GPTEditImage(图生图/垫图): prompt(必需)+image(URL/base64/本地路径/多图数组)+size+quality
- 文生图端点: /v1/images/generations (JSON)
- 图生图端点: /v1/images/edits (multipart/form-data，手写构建器!)
